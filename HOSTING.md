# Hosting your project under grom-labs

grom-labs is happy to host open-source projects for hardware people own — apps for FPV goggles,
tools, mods. The org gives your project a home next to related work; it does not take it over.

## You keep

- **Authorship and copyright.** Your name stays in the README and LICENSE.
- **Your license.** Any OSI-approved license, your choice. Hosting here never puts your code under toolya's AGPL.
- **Your donations.** Your repo's Sponsor button points to *you*, not to grom-labs.
- **Control.** You get the **Admin** role on your repo. Want to leave? Ask, and we transfer it back — no questions.

## We ask

1. **Your own code only.** No vendor firmware, binaries, leaked SDKs or keys. Dumps must be scrubbed of serials and keys.
2. **Hardware you own.** Interoperability and local development; nothing aimed at other people's devices or at bypassing safety limits (e.g. RF power/region locks).
3. **A `.github/FUNDING.yml` in your repo** — with your links, or an empty file if you don't want donations.
   Without it GitHub shows the org default (grom-labs' Ko-fi) on your project.
4. **A README** that says what it works with, how to install, and how to undo it.
5. Keep it alive or say it's archived — we may archive repos that are broken and silent for a year.

## How to join

Open an issue in [grom-labs/.github](https://github.com/grom-labs/.github/issues) with a link to your repo,
or start from [project-template/](project-template/). We transfer (or fork) it into the org and give you Admin.

Projects are listed on the [org page](https://github.com/grom-labs) as *hosted*, with the author's name.
